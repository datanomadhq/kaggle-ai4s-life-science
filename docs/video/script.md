# Demo video script and storyboard (target 4:30, max 5:00)

Production: slides rendered to PNG (`docs/video/build_video.py`), narration synthesised with macOS `say`
(voice "Samantha"), assembled with ffmpeg (1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC). Every image shown is produced by the
repository code from public CC0 data. No music (avoids licensing issues).

| 1 | 0:00-0:20 | Title card: VirtualPaint-Tox; category Model & Algorithm; team | "Drug-induced liver injury is the leading cause of drug withdrawal.
